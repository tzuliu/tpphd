 This paper finds strong evidence that involvement in sexual harassment scandals lowers candidate support to a greater degree than other types of scandals. It also finds that male voters tend to ease the negative effect of sexual harassment when the violators are male candidates.

Keywords: political scandals; sexual harassment; valence issues; candidate evaluations; conjoint analysis

Papers, data, and replication codes could be reached [here](https://github.com/tzuliu/The-Greater-Effects-of-Sexual-Harassment).

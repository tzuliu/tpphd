I received my Ph.D. from the [Department of Political Science](https://ps.ucdavis.edu/ "UCD PoliSci") at the [University of California, Davis](https://www.ucdavis.edu/ "UC Davis"), and I am currently an associate professor in the [Department of Public Affairs](https://public.utaipei.edu.tw/index.php?Lang=zh-tw) at the [University of Taipei](https://www.utaipei.edu.tw/index.php?Lang=zh-tw).

My research interests include: `Western European politics`, `causal inference`, `ideal point estimation`, `time-series analysis`, `machine learning`, `text analysis`, `NLP`, and `network analysis`.

All finalized and ongoing research projects — including data, papers, and replication files — can be found on my [GitHub page](https://github.com/tzuliu "tpliu github").

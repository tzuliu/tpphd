This is Tzu-Ping Liu. I am currently an associate professor in the [Department of Public Affairs](https://public.utaipei.edu.tw/index.php?Lang=zh-tw) at the [University of Taipei](https://www.utaipei.edu.tw/index.php?Lang=zh-tw). I received my Ph.D. from the [Department of Political Science](https://ps.ucdavis.edu/ "UCD PoliSci") at the [University of California, Davis](https://www.ucdavis.edu/ "UC Davis"). My research spans comparative politics and political methodology, with broader interests including Western European and Asian politics, causal inference, time-series and survival analysis, machine learning, computational linguistics, and network analysis. My work has been published in the ***European Journal of Political Research***, ***Japanese Journal of Political Science***, ***Party Politics***, and ***Public Choice***.
